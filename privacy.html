Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Mr. eSports respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used while operating the Mr. eSports application.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email address, username and team name.
  • Tournament information such as joined matches, slots, points, eliminations, final rank and rewards.
  • Wallet records such as coin requests, withdrawal requests and transaction status.
  • Information voluntarily submitted through support requests, including payment screenshots where required.
  • Basic technical information needed for authentication, database access and app functionality.

How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage accounts, operate tournaments, confirm slots, publish points tables, calculate standings, credit approved rewards, process requests, send notifications and protect the platform from misuse.

Firebase Services

Mr. eSports uses Firebase services such as Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage. These services may process information according to Google's applicable privacy terms.

Information Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may only be shared when necessary to operate the service, comply with applicable law, prevent fraud or protect users and the platform.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to protect account and tournament information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably required to provide app services, maintain tournament records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements.

Your Choices

Users may request assistance regarding their account or information through the Contact or Support section inside the app. Never share your password or OTP with anyone.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or requirements change. The latest update date will be displayed on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Mr. eSports through the Contact or Support section available inside the application.
